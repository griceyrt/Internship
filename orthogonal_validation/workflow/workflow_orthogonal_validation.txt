ORTHOGONAL VALIDATION WORKFLOW
================================
Goal: Validate splicing and DE events from the lab's Nanopore manuscript using
      public Illumina short-read RNA-seq data (GSE130613). Overlap between both
      technologies = orthogonal validation.

Dataset: GSE130613 (Manella & Asher, PNAS 2020) — normoxia constant-darkness liver
  NOTE: MARS-Seq 3' end sequencing — not ideal for splicing but approved by Bharath.
  NOTE: Original authors used STAR + HTSeq-count on mm10. We use Salmon + mm39.

Comparison: Liver WT CT16-20 (n=4) vs Liver PerDKO CT16-20 (n=4), constant darkness
  WT:     SRR9002567, SRR9002568, SRR9002569, SRR9002570
  PerDKO: SRR9002583, SRR9002584, SRR9002585, SRR9002586
  SRP accession: SRP194523

Reference genome: mm39 (GRCm39)
Custom transcriptome: transcriptome_ext.fa (Khushi) — ENSMUST + novel UUID transcripts
Custom GTF: transcriptome_productivity.gtf


==============================================================================
TRACK 1 — SPLICING ANALYSIS
==============================================================================

--- SETUP [BASH] -------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1a — Download raw FASTQ files from SRA
  8 samples, single-end (MARS-Seq 3' end)
  Script: scripts/get_sra_dump_fastq_run_salmon.sh
  Tools: prefetch + fasterq-dump (sra-tools)
  Output: temp/<SRR>.fastq.gz

STEP 1b — Adapter + poly-tail trimming (cutadapt)
  Flags: -a AGATCGGAAGAG -a "A{12}" -a "T{12}" -a "G{12}" --minimum-length 20
  Rationale: Illumina adapter + polyA/T tails + polyG artifact (NextSeq 500 two-color)
  Output: temp/<SRR>_trimmed_full.fastq.gz

STEP 2 — Build Salmon index (once, already built)
  Input: transcriptome_ext.fa + mm39 genome FASTA → gentrome_ext.fa + decoys.txt
  Command:
    salmon index -t data/gentrome_ext.fa \
                 -i data/transcriptome_ext_index \
                 --decoys data/decoys.txt \
                 -k 31 --keepDuplicates
  Index: data/transcriptome_ext_index/

--- SALMON [BASH] ------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 3 — Salmon quantification (all 8 samples)
  Script: scripts/get_sra_dump_fastq_run_salmon.sh SRP194523 data/transcriptome_ext_index
  Mode: quasi-mapping, single-end (-r), library auto-detect (-l A)
  Salmon detected: -l U (unstranded) — confirmed correct
  Flags: --seqBias --gcBias
  Output: results/SRP194523_salmon_2026-06-20/<SRR>/quant.sf

  Mapping rates (after full trimming):
    SRR9002567 WT:     82.32%   (was 56.71%)
    SRR9002568 WT:     82.28%   (was 57.22%)
    SRR9002569 WT:     87.86%   (was 70.17%)
    SRR9002570 WT:     82.22%   (was 56.89%)
    SRR9002583 PerDKO: 76.36%   (was 47.26%)
    SRR9002584 PerDKO: 75.35%   (was 46.62%)
    SRR9002585 PerDKO: 73.00%   (was 43.82%)
    SRR9002586 PerDKO: 76.43%   (was 47.54%)

  *** BRANCHING POINT ***
  -> Track 1 continues at STEP 7
  -> Track 2 starts at STEP 5a (run in parallel)

--- SPLICING ANALYSIS [BASH / PYTHON] ----------------------------------------

STEP 7 — generateEvents from GTF
  suppa.py generateEvents -i transcriptome_productivity.gtf \
                          -o results/suppa_<date>/transcriptome_ext \
                          -f ioe -e SE SS MX RI FL -b S
  Output: IOE files per event type (SE, A3, A5, MX, RI, AF, AL)
  Boundary: -b S (strict) — confirmed by manuscript and Khushi

STEP 8 — Normalised expression table (input for SUPPA)
  Input: counts normalised in Step 5c (NOT raw Salmon TPM or counts)
  Output: results/normalisation/combined_norm.tab
  NOTE: Batch correction SKIPPED — no batch info available for GSE130613

STEP 9 — psiPerEvent
  suppa.py psiPerEvent -i <event.ioe> \
                       -e combined_norm.tab \
                       -o results/suppa_<date>/
  Output: .psi files per event type

STEP 10a — Split PSI and expression table by condition
  *_WT.psi / *_KO.psi  (SRR9002567–70 vs SRR9002583–86)
  WT_norm.tab / KO_norm.tab

STEP 10b — diffSplice (WT vs PerDKO)
  suppa.py diffSplice --method empirical \
                      --input <event.ioe> \
                      --psi *_WT.psi *_KO.psi \
                      --tpm WT_norm.tab KO_norm.tab \
                      -gc -o results/suppa_<date>/diff/diff_<EVENT>
  Output: diff_*.dpsi.temp.0
  Significance filter (Illumina): |dPSI| > 0.1 AND p < 0.3
    NOTE: relaxed from 0.05 — low power at n=4 with empirical method
  Sign convention: dPSI output = WT − KO; negated in plots to show KO − WT


==============================================================================
TRACK 2 — DIFFERENTIAL EXPRESSION
==============================================================================

--- NORMALISATION [R] --------------------------------------------------------

STEP 5a — Import Salmon output into R
  Script: scripts/normalisation_deseq2.R
  tximport reads all 8 quant.sf at transcript level (txOut = TRUE)
  tx2gene built from transcriptome_productivity.gtf

STEP 5b — Filter
  Keep transcripts with >5 counts in ≥2 samples
  155,263 → 20,754 transcripts retained

STEP 5c — Normalise
  counts / total reads per sample
  Output: results/normalisation/combined_norm.tab  (SUPPA input, Steps 8–10b)

--- DESEQ2 + STAGER [R] ------------------------------------------------------

STEP 6 — DESeq2 differential expression + stageR
  Script: scripts/normalisation_deseq2.R
  Input: raw tximport counts (NOT normalised values)
  Two-stage testing:
    Stage 1 (gene): DESeq2 gene-level screen — 487 significant genes
    Stage 2 (tx):   stageR per-transcript confirmation (perGeneQValueHack)
  Significance: padj_gene_stageR < 0.05 AND |log2FC| > 0.5 → 801 significant transcripts
  Tighter threshold (matching Khushi's): padj < 0.05 AND |log2FC| > 1.5 → 383 transcripts
  Output: results/normalisation/deseq2_KO_vs_WT.tsv
  -> Feeds into Steps 13 (DE volcano), 14 (DE vs splicing Venn), 15 (DE concordance)


==============================================================================
RESULTS  [PYTHON — matplotlib / pandas]
==============================================================================

STEP 11 — dPSI Volcano plot (KO − WT)
  Script: scripts/plot_results.py
  X-axis: dPSI (KO − WT, sign negated from SUPPA output)
  Y-axis: −log10(p-value)
  Thresholds marked: |dPSI| > 0.1, p < 0.3  (horizontal line at −log10(0.3))
  Output: results/figures/step11_dPSI_volcano.png
  514 significant splicing events

STEP 12 — Splicing concordance: Illumina vs Nanopore
  Script: scripts/crossreference.py
  Match on exact event ID between Illumina (|dPSI|>0.1, p<0.3) and Nanopore (|dPSI|>0.1, p<0.05)
  Nanopore data: data/nanopore_suppa/ (Khushi's res_*.dpsi.temp.0 files)
  Output: results/figures/step12_dPSI_scatter.png
          results/tables/step12_overlap_events.xlsx
  32 overlapping splicing events (orange); 3 validated at strict p<0.05

STEP 13 — DE Volcano plot (PerDKO vs WT)
  Script: scripts/plot_results.py
  X-axis: log2FC (KO − WT); Y-axis: −log10(padj_gene_stageR)
  Thresholds: |log2FC| > 0.5, padj < 0.05
  Gene labels selected by Kiran (circadian, Cyp450/metabolism, immune — ~27 genes)
  Output: results/figures/step13_DE_volcano.png

STEP 14 — DE vs Splicing Venn
  Script: scripts/plot_results.py
  DE transcripts (stageR, |log2FC|>0.5) vs Illumina spliced genes (|dPSI|>0.1, p<0.3)
  Output: results/figures/step14_DE_vs_splicing_venn.png
  Tables: results/tables/step14_DE_only.xlsx
          results/tables/step14_DE_and_spliced.xlsx
          results/tables/step14_spliced_only.xlsx

STEP 15 — DE concordance: Illumina vs Nanopore (transcript level)
  Script: scripts/crossreference.py
  Match on transcript_id (NOT gene_id — transcript level preserves isoform resolution)
  Both sides threshold: padj < 0.05 AND |log2FC| > 1.5 (matching Khushi's manuscript)
  Includes both ENSMUST and UUID novel transcripts (shared via same custom transcriptome)
  Background: 12,966 shared transcripts (present in both datasets)
  Illumina significant: 383 transcripts
  Nanopore significant: 267 transcripts (Khushi's pre-computed, Table3 perKO_sig sheet)
  Overlap: 16 transcripts (11 annotated ENSMUST + 5 novel UUID); 11/16 consistent direction
  Khushi's 267 fully accounted for: 16 (both) + 92 (Nanopore only) + 18 (NA padj) + 141 (zero reads in Illumina) = 267
  Output: results/figures/step15_DE_concordance_scatter_v2.png
          results/tables/step15_DE_overlap_Illumina_vs_Nanopore.xlsx
          results/tables/step15_khushi_267_transcript_breakdown.xlsx
